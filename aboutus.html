About Us

VisionEdge Group

Creating Platforms. Connecting Markets. Enabling Growth.

VisionEdge Group is an exhibition and business-platform company creating focused trade fairs, exhibitions and industry-led platforms that bring businesses, professionals, buyers and innovators together.

Our journey in exhibitions began in 2010 with an exhibition in Pune, followed by three editions in Kolhapur in 2011, 2012 and 2013. Building on this experience, VisionEdge Group is now entering a new phase — developing professionally curated exhibitions across industrial, business and emerging sectors.

We believe a successful exhibition is more than an event. It is a marketplace for ideas, products, relationships and opportunities. Our exhibitions are designed to connect the right audiences with the right businesses, create meaningful face-to-face interactions and open new avenues for business development, market access, networking and growth.

Our Vision

Where we're headed

To build a trusted portfolio of exhibitions and business platforms that connect markets, inspire participation and create opportunities for sustainable growth.

Our Mission

How we get there

To conceptualise and deliver focused, professionally managed exhibitions that create measurable value for exhibitors, visitors, partners and the markets we serve.

We achieve this through

Five principles behind every platform we build

RELEVANCE

Understanding each sector and creating exhibitions around its specific market needs.

CONNECTION

Bringing businesses, buyers, professionals and communities together.

EXPERIENCE

Creating engaging, professional and high-quality exhibition environments.

OPPORTUNITY

Enabling business development, networking, market expansion and new partnerships.

INNOVATION

Continuously evolving our exhibitions with changing markets, technologies and consumer needs.

Every Market Has a Story. Every Exhibition Should Create an Opportunity.

From industrial technology and manufacturing to new and emerging sectors, VisionEdge Group aims to develop purpose-built platforms with a clear market proposition, defined audience and strong business potential.

We combine market understanding, strategic promotion, industry partnerships and professional exhibition management to create platforms that deliver value beyond the exhibition floor.

Our Approach

Purpose-built platforms, backed by market understanding and professional exhibition management.
